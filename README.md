### Why I build
I've always felt that a codebase is the one thing I can have total control over. When I'm writing code, I'm not just making something that works, I'm designing a world where the architecture, the flow, and every little detail follows a plan I've set. It's about that sense of clarity you get when a system is resilient and does exactly what it's supposed to do.

I'm less interested in just "building apps" and more interested in building things that actually have meaning. If a system doesn't have a purpose or a creative edge, it's just syntax. I like things that feel premium and solid under the hood.

---

### Lately
Lately I've been pushing myself out of my comfort zone. I spent a long time in the web and blockchain space, but now I'm shifting gears a bit.

I'm currently getting deep into **Desktop development**. There's something about building native apps that feel fast and responsive that really appeals to my need for performance. I want to see how far I can take my existing stack into the native world.

I'm also exploring **3D programming**. I've been diving into **Three.js** and **WebGL** because I'm fascinated by the intersection of math, art, and interaction. It's a completely different way of thinking about the "frontend," and it's been one of my favorite rabbit holes recently. Redefining what "interaction" means is part of the mission.
